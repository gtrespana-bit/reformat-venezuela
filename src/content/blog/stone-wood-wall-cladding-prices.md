## Introduction: Walls That Make a Difference

Beyond paint, decorative wall cladding has become one of the most requested upgrades in Venezuelan remodels. It adds texture, warmth and a premium look without having to remodel the entire room.

At RemodelaT Venezuela we install stone, wood, panels and microcement in homes and offices in **Caracas, Valencia and Carabobo**. Here is a practical 2026 guide with real prices.

---

## Main Options and Prices (installed per m²)

| Material                    | Price Range (USD/m²) | Best For                     | Maintenance |
|-----------------------------|----------------------|------------------------------|-------------|
| **Natural stone (travertine, slate)** | $45 - $90       | Feature walls, exteriors     | Medium      |
| **Manufactured stone**      | $28 - $55            | Living rooms, fireplaces     | Low         |
| **Wood panels / slats**     | $35 - $70            | Bedrooms, offices, accent walls | Medium   |
| **MDF or PVC decorative panels** | $18 - $35       | Budget projects, quick impact| Very low    |
| **Microcement**             | $45 - $85            | Wet areas, modern look       | Very low    |
| **3D panels / gypsum**      | $22 - $40            | Budget feature walls         | Low         |

---

## Recommendations by Space

- **Living room / TV wall**: Wood slats or manufactured stone
- **Bedroom headboard**: Wood panels or upholstered
- **Bathroom / wet area**: Microcement or porcelain
- **Hallway / entrance**: Stone or 3D panels
- **Office**: Clean wood or microcement

---

## Tips

- Always calculate 10-15% extra for cuts and waste.
- In humid areas, avoid natural wood without proper treatment.
- Microcement and manufactured stone offer the best durability/price ratio for most homes.
