## Introduction: The Question Everyone Asks

“How long will the work take?” is the first question after the price. And the honest answer is: it depends. But that doesn’t help you plan your life, so in this guide we give you **real timelines by stage** based on hundreds of projects executed in **Caracas, Valencia, San Diego, and Carabobo**.

A well-planned full remodel has predictable times. Delays almost always come from three sources: late client decisions, materials that don’t arrive, or poor company planning. Knowing how time is divided helps you demand and anticipate.

## Stages of a Full Remodel and Their Duration

### 1. Design and planning (1-3 weeks, before work)
Survey, plans, material selection, and closed budget. This stage is not counted as “work,” but it is the one that prevents the most delays later. A project without defined plans inevitably stretches.

![Remodel project plan and design](/images/integrales-plano-diseno.webp)

### 2. Demolition and debris removal (3-7 days)
Chipping walls, lifting floors, removing old fixtures and kitchen, hauling debris. It is the noisiest and dirtiest stage. In condominiums there are restricted hours that can extend it.

![Demolition in full remodel](/images/integrales-demolicion.webp)

### 3. Masonry and structure (1-3 weeks)
Building new walls, patching, leveling floors, preparing surfaces. It is the foundation of everything else.

### 4. Installations: electrical and plumbing (1-2 weeks)
Rewiring, new pipes, panel, water points, and drains. This stage happens inside the walls, so it must finish before closing. To understand its scope, see our guides on [electrical installation](/en/blog/electrical-installation-prices-venezuela) and [plumbing](/en/blog/plumbing-prices-caracas).

### 5. Wall and floor coverings (1-3 weeks)
Installing porcelain, ceramic, wall coverings. Depends on the surface area and material. Large-format porcelain is slower than standard ceramic.

### 6. Carpentry and furniture (1-3 weeks)
Kitchen, closets, doors, custom furniture. Often fabricated in parallel while other stages advance, but installation is at the end.

### 7. Painting and finishes (1-2 weeks)
Painting, microcement, final details. It is one of the last stages because everything previous generates dust.

### 8. Final installation and cleaning (3-7 days)
Fixtures, faucets, lighting, appliances, hardware. Deep cleaning and handover.

## What Factors Extend a Project

### 1. Late decisions
The number one delay. If the client takes weeks to choose the floor, kitchen, or colors, the work stops. **Tip:** make all material decisions before starting.

### 2. Imported or made-to-order materials
An imported porcelain or custom kitchen can take 4-8 weeks to arrive. If ordered late, it paralyzes the end of the project.

### 3. On-the-fly changes
“Move this wall,” “change the bathroom location.” Every change means redesigning, re-quoting, and redoing. Changes during construction are the enemy of the timeline.

### 4. Condominium permits
In apartments, work hours and permits can limit effective days. In our article on [permits for remodeling in condominiums](/en/blog/remodel-permits-apartment-condominium) we explain how to manage them.

### 5. Poor company planning
A company that doesn’t coordinate stages well (for example, painting before finishing installations) loses valuable days. Correct sequencing is key.

## How to Speed Up Your Remodel (Without Sacrificing Quality)

- **Define everything before starting:** materials, colors, layout
- **Purchase materials in advance**, especially imports
- **Hire a company that coordinates all stages** (avoid hiring each trade separately)
- **Respect the sequence:** don’t ask to skip stages
- **Weekly communication:** a progress meeting prevents misunderstandings

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I live in the house during a full remodel?
In partial remodels (kitchen or bathroom), yes, with inconvenience. In a full remodel, it is recommended to move temporarily: there will be days without water, without electricity, and lots of dust.

### Why did my neighbor finish in 6 weeks and they tell me 12?
Because the scope is not the same. A “cosmetic” remodel (paint and floors) is much faster than a full one that touches installations and layout. Compare equivalent projects.

### Does a shorter timeline mean a better company?
No. A realistic and met timeline is worth more than an optimistic promise that is not fulfilled. Be suspicious of anyone who promises to finish in half the reasonable time.

### What happens if the company is delayed?
A good contract includes the timeline and sometimes penalties for unjustified delay. That’s why a clear contract is so important (see [how to choose a remodeling company](/en/blog/how-to-choose-remodeling-company-venezuela)).
