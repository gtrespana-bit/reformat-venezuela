## Introduction: The Price per m² That Nobody Explains Well

When asking for a painting quote, the most common answer is "it depends on the m²." But what does that really mean? Many people receive vague numbers and later discover that "per m²" does not include preparation, primer, number of coats, or protection of furniture.

At ReformaT Venezuela we have painted thousands of square meters in **Caracas, Valencia, San Diego and Carabobo**. Here are the **real 2026 prices**, broken down.

---

## What Really Affects the Price

1. **Condition of the walls** — Old paint, cracks, humidity or previous poor jobs require more preparation.
2. **Number of coats** — Quality work almost always requires primer + 2 coats.
3. **Type of paint** — Economy, washable, anti-mold, low odor, etc.
4. **Access and height** — High ceilings or difficult areas increase cost.
5. **Protection and cleaning** — Professional service protects furniture and leaves everything clean.

---

## How to Calculate Your Budget

Example: 120 m² apartment (typical 2-3 bedrooms)

- Walls only (standard quality): 120 m² × $13 = **$1,560**
- Walls + ceilings: ≈ **$2,100 - $2,600**
- Full premium (better paint + full prep): **$2,800 - $3,800**

Always ask for a quote with exact square meters and a detailed breakdown.

---

## Recommendations

- Never accept "per m²" without knowing how many coats and what preparation is included.
- In humid areas (bathrooms, kitchens), use at least washable or anti-mold paint.
- Good preparation is more important than the paint brand.
