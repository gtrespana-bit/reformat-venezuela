## What Does a Full Remodel Include?

A full remodel covers **all or most** of these works:

### 1. Demolition and Rough Work
- Removal of floors, coverings, fixtures, existing kitchen
- Demolition of non-structural walls (for new layout)
- Debris removal and cleaning
- **Cost:** $8-$15/m²

### 2. Plumbing Installations
- Cold and hot water pipes (PPR or copper)
- Drains and waste stacks
- Water points for kitchen, bathrooms, service area
- **Cost:** $15-$30/m² (depending on number of bathrooms and points)

### 3. Electrical Installations
- Complete wiring (copper, Covenin standard)
- New electrical panel with breakers
- Light points, outlets, switches
- Dedicated circuits (air conditioning, kitchen, washer)
- **Cost:** $12-$25/m²

### 4. Flooring and Wall Coverings
- Floor leveling
- Installation of porcelain, marble or wood
- Wall coverings in bathrooms and kitchen
- **Cost:** $25-$80/m² (depending on material)

### 5. Kitchen
- Design and fabrication of cabinets
- Countertop (granite, quartz, porcelain)
- Appliances (optional)
- **Cost:** $3,500-$12,000 (depending on size and materials)

### 6. Bathrooms
- Waterproofing
- Fixtures, faucets, shower
- Bathroom vanities, mirrors
- **Cost:** $2,500-$8,000 per bathroom

### 7. Painting and Finishes
- Plastering and sanding of walls
- Interior painting (2-3 coats)
- Moldings, baseboards, cornices
- **Cost:** $6-$15/m² of wall

### 8. Carpentry and Closets
- Interior doors
- Closets and walk-in closets
- Built-in furniture
- **Cost:** $1,500-$6,000 (depending on quantity)

### 9. Lighting
- Lighting design
- Installation of LED points, spotlights, indirect lighting
- **Cost:** $800-$3,000

### 10. Air Conditioning (optional)
- Air conditioning points
- Ducts (if applicable)
- **Cost:** $500-$2,000 per point

## Real Timelines for a Full Remodel

| Size                  | Estimated Time     | Phases |
|-----------------------|--------------------|--------|
| 60-80 m² apartment    | 6-10 weeks         | 4 phases |
| 100-150 m² apartment  | 10-16 weeks        | 5 phases |
| 200-300 m² house      | 4-6 months         | 6 phases |
| Premium house +300 m² | 6-9 months         | 6-7 phases |

### Typical schedule (100 m² apartment):

**Weeks 1-2:** Demolition, debris removal, structural inspection.

**Weeks 3-5:** Installations (plumbing, electrical, air conditioning). Pressure and continuity tests.

**Weeks 6-9:** Rough work: floors, coverings, waterproofing, plastering.

**Weeks 10-12:** Finishes: painting, carpentry, kitchen, bathrooms, lighting.

**Weeks 13-14:** Final details, deep cleaning, handover.

## Mistakes That Make a Full Remodel More Expensive

### 1. No plans or previous design
Starting construction without a defined plan generates changes on the fly. Every change costs 2-3x more than if planned from the beginning.

### 2. Choosing materials during construction
If the floor is not purchased when installation begins, the team stops. Stopped days = money lost. All materials must be purchased before starting.

### 3. Not budgeting for contingency
Every full remodel has surprises: a corroded pipe, a wall that is not plumb, an uneven slab. Reserve a **15-20% contingency** on the budget.

### 4. Hiring separately (plumber, electrician, mason)
Coordinating 5 independent contractors is a logistical nightmare. Timelines double, warranties dilute and no one is responsible for the other's work. A full-service company is responsible for everything.

### 5. Not verifying condominium rules
In buildings in **Chacao, Altamira, La Castellana and La Lagunita**, condominium boards require:
- Liability insurance ($200-$500)
- Security deposit ($500-$2,000)
- Restricted hours (8 AM - 5 PM)
- Worker access through parking
- Plan approved by building engineer

Not complying can stop the work for weeks.

## Premium Zones: Special Considerations

### La Lagunita / Country Club / Alto Hatillo
300-600 m² houses with strict urbanization rules. Remodels here usually include:
- Space expansions (requires municipal engineering permit)
- Integration of social areas (kitchen-living-dining)
- Basic home automation (lighting, climate control)
- Budgets starting at $50,000

### Altamira / La Castellana / Campo Alegre
120-250 m² apartments in buildings with demanding regulations:
- Time and access limitations
- Prohibition of modifying facade
- Board approval for layout changes
- Budgets starting at $30,000

### Guataparo / El Viñedo / Prebo (Carabobo)
Houses and townhouses with more construction freedom:
- Fewer condominium restrictions
- Possibility of expansion
- Costs 10-15% lower than Caracas
- Budgets starting at $25,000
