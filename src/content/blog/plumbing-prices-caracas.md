## What Professional Plumbing Work Includes

Serious sanitary work is not “connecting pipes.” It includes:

- **Diameter calculations** according to required flow and pressure
- **Proper piping** (PVC, CPVC, PEX, or copper depending on use)
- **Certified welding or joints** with leak testing
- **Correct slopes** in drains (minimum 2% to prevent clogs)
- **Sanitary ventilation** to prevent siphoning and bad odors
- **Pressure test** before closing walls
- **Written warranty** on labor

## Factors That Change the Price

### 1. Pipe accessibility
A leak under a visible sink costs a fraction of one inside a concrete wall. The more difficult the access, the more labor hours and the more subsequent repair work.

### 2. Pipe material
PEX and CPVC are faster to install and corrosion-resistant. Copper is more expensive but very durable. Old galvanized iron pipes often require complete replacement.

### 3. Urgency
Scheduled work always costs less than a midnight emergency. If you can plan, you save.

### 4. Collateral damage
If the leak has already damaged walls, floors, or ceilings, the plumbing repair is only part of it. Patching, painting, and material replacement add up.

## Signs You Have a Plumbing Problem

Don’t wait for a flood. These signs deserve attention:

- Moisture stains or bubbled paint on walls and ceilings
- Persistent musty or mold smell
- Water bill rising without explanation
- Low water pressure at faucets or shower
- Slow drains or bad odors
- Sound of running water when everything is closed
