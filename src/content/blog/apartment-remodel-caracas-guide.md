## Introduction: Remodeling an Apartment in Caracas

Remodeling an apartment in Caracas has specific challenges that don't exist in houses: condominium rules, limited access, logistics of materials through elevators, neighbor relations, and older buildings with outdated installations.

At ReformaT Venezuela we have completed hundreds of apartment remodels in **Chacao, Altamira, La Castellana, Campo Alegre, Los Naranjos, Prados del Este and other areas** of Caracas. This guide summarizes the key points for 2026.

## Main Challenges in Caracas Apartments

1. **Condominium regulations** — Almost always require written authorization, insurance and deposit.
2. **Access and hours** — Limited elevator hours, service entrance only, strict 8am-5pm schedules.
3. **Old installations** — Many buildings from the 70s-90s have aluminum wiring and old plumbing.
4. **Debris removal** — Must be bagged and taken down manually in many buildings.
5. **Noise and neighbors** — Very sensitive in premium areas.

---

## Recommended Approach

- Hire a company experienced in Caracas buildings (they already know the rules of each one).
- Have all materials on site before starting.
- Plan for 15-20% contingency.
- Get everything in writing with the condominium from day one.
