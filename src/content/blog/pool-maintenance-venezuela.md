## Weekly Maintenance Routine (Do It Yourself)

| Task                        | Frequency     | Time     | Why it matters |
|-----------------------------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Skim leaves and debris      | Daily         | 5 min    | Prevents filter clogging |
| Brush walls and floor       | 2-3 times/week| 15-20 min| Prevents algae |
| Check pH and chlorine       | 2-3 times/week| 5 min    | Health and equipment protection |
| Empty skimmer and pump basket| Weekly       | 5 min    | Maintains water flow |
| Backwash filter             | Weekly or when pressure rises | 10 min | Keeps filtration efficient |

**Basic chemistry (target values):**
- pH: 7.2 – 7.6
- Free chlorine: 1 – 3 ppm
- Alkalinity: 80 – 120 ppm
- Calcium hardness: 200 – 400 ppm

## Most Common Problems and Solutions

### 1. Green water (algae)
**Cause:** Low chlorine + high phosphates + lack of brushing.  
**Solution:** Shock treatment + algaecide + brush walls + run filter 24h. Cost: $80-180.

### 2. Cloudy water
**Cause:** Poor filtration, high pH, or dead algae.  
**Solution:** Check filter, adjust chemistry, flocculant if needed.

### 3. Scale on tiles and equipment
**Cause:** High calcium + high pH.  
**Solution:** Lower pH gradually and use scale remover. In hard water areas (parts of Valencia), use sequestering agents.

### 4. Pump not working or noisy
**Cause:** Debris in impeller, bad capacitor, or air leak.  
**Prevention:** Keep baskets clean and check o-rings.

## Professional Maintenance vs DIY

**Do it yourself if:**
- You have time every week
- You learn basic chemistry
- The pool is small and simple

**Hire professionals if:**
- You travel frequently
- The pool is large or has complex equipment (salt chlorinator, heater, automation)
- You want warranty on chemicals and work
- You had repeated problems in the past

## Seasonal Tips for Venezuela

- **Rainy season (May-Nov):** Increase chlorine and check pH more often. Rain dilutes chemicals.
- **Dry season (Dec-Apr):** Watch evaporation and calcium levels.
- **After long inactivity:** Full opening service is essential (never just add chlorine to dirty water).
